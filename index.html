Our Story

Made in Rajasthan,
Worn Across India

The Baisa is a Jaipur-based women's garment brand rooted in the craftsmanship tradition of Sanganer. Every piece is manufactured in our own unit with Jack stitching machines, skilled karigars, and 3-stage quality control.

Our mission: make high-quality ethnic fashion accessible to every Indian woman, city or town, size S or 5XL.
